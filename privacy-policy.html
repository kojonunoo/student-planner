Student Planner Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026

Student Planner is provided by Kojo Nunoo. Contact: emmanuel.nunoo@outlook.com.

Your data stays on your device

Your subjects, timetable, homework, exams, revision sessions, flashcards, review progress, saved PDFs, display name and settings are stored only on your device. Student Planner has no account system, no developer server and no cloud sync. The Android version does not request internet access, so the app cannot send your data anywhere. Device backup and transfer services can keep app data according to your device settings.

Files you choose

When you add a PDF, Student Planner copies the file you pick into its own storage on your device. When you export a backup, the app writes a file to the location you choose; when you import one, it reads only the file you pick. Backup files contain your planner data and PDFs, so keep them somewhere private.

Reminders

If you turn on notifications, reminders for lessons, revision, homework, exams and daily practice are scheduled locally by your device. Student Planner asks to restart reminders after your device reboots. No reminder information leaves your device.

Analytics and advertising

Student Planner contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs and does not sell or share your data.

Children

Student Planner is intended for users aged 13 and over.

Retention and deletion

Data remains until you delete it in the app, clear Student Planner's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. This does not delete backup files you exported or device backups.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
